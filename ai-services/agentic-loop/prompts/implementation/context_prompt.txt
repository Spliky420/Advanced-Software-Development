How to read the evidence below:

- A "DATABASE REVIEW TARGET" section contains that student's Dockerfile,
  init.sql, and seed.sql for the database layer.
- An "IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW TARGET" section contains that student's
  backend app.py and frontend app.py for the API/UI layer.
- Evidence for more than one student may be concatenated, one section per
  student -- review each student's evidence independently and do not
  compare students against each other.
- "FILE NOT FOUND" or "ERROR READING" in a section means that file is
  genuinely missing or unreadable -- treat it as a finding, not as
  evidence to skip silently.
